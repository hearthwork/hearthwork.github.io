Screen-Free Road Trip Activities for Kids 4 to 12 (Printable Pack)

Long drives do not have to mean hours of tablets or "are we there yet." A few simple games and a little planning keep kids busy and make the drive part of the adventure.

Pack an activity bag

Each kid gets a clipboard, pencils, crayons, a small toy, and a few printables. Hand out something new every hour or at each stop.

Play road-trip bingo

Kids spot cows, bridges, red cars, and water towers. Five in a row wins a small prize, like picking the next snack.

Do a car scavenger hunt

Look for things like "something purple," "a letter Q," or "three trucks in a row." It works for pre-readers too if you read the list aloud.

Track license plates

See how many states your family can find. Older kids can look up facts about each state at the next stop.

Keep a travel journal

Ask kids to draw one thing they saw each day and write the funniest thing that happened. It becomes a keepsake.

Use a countdown

A countdown chart with one box per hour or stop answers "how much longer" without you saying a word.

Classic no-supply games

I Spy, 20 Questions, the alphabet game, and story chains where everyone adds one sentence.
