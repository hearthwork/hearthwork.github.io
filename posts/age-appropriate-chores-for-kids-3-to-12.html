Age-Appropriate Chores for Kids 3 to 12 (Free Weekly Chore Chart)

Kids are more capable than we think, and helping at home builds confidence. The trick is matching chores to age and keeping the system simple enough that you actually use it.

Chores for ages 3 to 5

Put toys in a bin, feed the pet with help, match socks, wipe the table, put clothes in the hamper. Expect messy results. The habit matters more than the outcome.

Chores for ages 6 to 8

Make the bed, set and clear the table, sort laundry by color, water plants, pack their own school bag.

Chores for ages 9 to 12

Load the dishwasher, vacuum one room, fold and put away laundry, take out trash and recycling, help cook one meal a week.

How to teach a new chore

  1. Show them while they watch.
  2. Do it together.
  3. Let them do it while you watch.
  4. Let them do it alone. Praise effort, not perfection.

Should you pay for chores?

Many families split it: some chores are "because we live here," and a few extra jobs can earn money. Pick what fits your values and be consistent.

Make it visible

A chart on the fridge does the reminding for you. Kids like checking boxes, and you get to stop repeating yourself.
