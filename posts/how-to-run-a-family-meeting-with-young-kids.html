How to Run a Weekly Family Meeting With Young Kids (Free Agenda Printable)

Family meetings sound formal, but the best ones are short, a little silly, and happen at the same time every week. Here is a simple way to start, even if your kids are 3 and 9.

Why a weekly family meeting helps

Kids feel calmer when they know what is coming. A 15 minute check-in gives everyone a voice, cuts down on nagging, and turns daily friction into problems you solve together.

Step 1: Pick a time and keep it

Sunday evening after dinner works for many families. Keep it under 20 minutes. Younger kids can sit on a lap or hold a "talking spoon."

Step 2: Start with appreciations

Go around the table and have each person say one thing they liked about someone else this week. It sets a warm tone and kids love hearing it.

Step 3: Check the calendar

Walk through the week: practices, appointments, who needs a ride, special days. Kids handle changes better when they hear about them early.

Step 4: Solve one problem together

Choose one issue only, like "mornings are rushed." Let kids suggest ideas first. Write down what you decide and who does what.

Step 5: End with fun

Plan a small treat: a game night, pancakes Saturday, a walk to the park. This is what keeps kids coming back.

Tips for keeping it going

  • Let older kids take turns leading.
  • Keep notes in one place so you can check last week's decisions.
  • Skip a week if you need to, then just start again.
